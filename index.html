About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the University of Maryland, Baltimore County (UMBC), advised by Dr. Tim Oates in the CoRaL Lab. I was admitted to candidacy in February 2026 and will graduate on December 18, 2026 (GPA 3.933/4.0).

My dissertation studies low-resource Vietnamese handwriting recognition: a new three-level dataset and a multi-path architecture with fusion that generalizes to 3D CT report generation and mathematical reasoning of large language models.

Research projects

CoRaL Lab, UMBC · 2023 to present

MDEF: Multi-Report Deep Ensemble Fusion

3D CT report generation · First author · ICDM 2026 workshop
  • Turned the single-report paradigm of 3D CT report generation into a multi-report deep ensemble.
  • Beat MARCH, the best single-report baseline, by +0.079 and +0.045 CE F1 on RadGenome and CT-RATE, with a small positive margin on INSPECT.
  • Raised recall by 70% to 97% relative to MARCH, averaged over 18 pathologies.
Code

MDToC: Metacognitive Dynamic Tree of Concepts

LLM mathematical reasoning · First author · ICT Research
  • Three-phase prompting built on the metacognitive stages of planning, monitoring, and reviewing.
  • Beat Graph of Thoughts by 5% on CHAMP, 5.4% on MATH, and 4% on Game of 24.
  • Beat Tree of Thoughts by up to 19% and Graph of Thoughts by up to 13% across backbone models.
Code arXiv

ViTLD: Three-Level Vietnamese Handwriting Dataset

Dataset · First author · Under review at WACV 2027
  • Built from 565 photographed pages by 435 writers: the first Vietnamese handwriting dataset with word, line, and paragraph labels on photographed paper.
  • Harder than VNOnDB and UIT-HWDB: average word-level CER of nine recognizers rises to 12.56 (from 7.32 and 9.39).
  • Harder at line level too: average CER of 22.57, versus 14.09 on VNOnDB and 16.91 on UIT-HWDB.

SIME: Stroke-Image Modality Ensemble

Handwriting recognition with VLMs · First author · In revision
  • Adds a stroke modality, reconstructed from the image by stroke derendering, to an ensemble of fine-tuned vision-language models.
  • Beat the strongest image-only ensemble on two out-of-distribution benchmarks, cutting CER by about 11% on VN-Para and 9% on VN-Addr.
  • Released VN-Para, a diacritic-rich word-level benchmark of 8,042 handwritten word images from 100 documents.

PIKE: Knowledge Graph Enrichment for ICU Outcomes

Medical AI · Contributing author · Under review at AAAI 2027
  • Built on Evidence-Guided Node Selection (EGNS) and Bidirectional Low-Rank Fusion (BLRF).
  • Beat the strongest baseline by 2.2 to 4.0 AUROC points in each setting on MIMIC-III and MIMIC-IV.
  • Improved AUPRC by up to 6.9 points over CTPD on MIMIC-IV mortality.
